### 3. Ukraine sinks a Russian warship off Libya — REPORTED — HERO
**Reuters / AP · Kyiv.** Zelenskyy announces on Monday that seven
Ukrainian sea drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury in Benghazi
on Sunday; the cargo ship MV Angara was hit too, and a Ka-27
helicopter destroyed — 2,400 km from the front. (The attack was
early Sunday — the Monday news is the announcement and reactions:
Kremlin "terrorist activity" brief 16, Tripoli's "grave concern"
brief 36, refuelling-deal brief 28.)

### 1. COP31 ends with no deal — the longest summit ever — REPORTED
**UN News / BBC · Antalya.** COP31 ends with no pact on Monday after
a record 44 hours of overtime — the longest climate summit ever, and
the first with no deal since 2000. Presidency chief Kurum concedes
defeat at Sunday's closing plenary; the EU and 130 nations rejected
a draft text with no fossil-fuel language. Guterres speaks on
Monday, "deeply disappointed". (UN News published 05:28 UTC Monday.)

### 2. NATO in emergency session over Poland — REPORTED
**Reuters / RMF24 · Brussels.** The North Atlantic Council holds an
emergency session in Brussels on Monday over three Russian Kh-101
cruise missiles that fell in Poland on Friday 20 November. Poland
asks for stronger eastern-flank air defences; Rutte says NATO "will
defend every inch" of allied territory. (The missiles fell Friday —
the Monday news is the emergency session.)

## Cards (4–13)

### 4. Hormuz attacks hit a wartime high — REPORTED
**CNN / UKMTO.** 22 attacks in November, 31 in October — the most
since the war began in February. UKMTO's threat index for the strait
is CRITICAL (refreshed 09:01 UTC Monday); Iran's navy says two
tankers hit mines (04:07 UTC Monday).

### 5. Gaza now a 'toxic wasteland', Oxfam warns — REPORTED
**Oxfam / CEOBS.** A report published on Monday finds contamination
indicators across over 99% of Gaza and 78% of its land affected.

### 6. Cikande toll confirmed at 24; ~30 missing — REPORTED
**Reuters · Indonesia.** The candy-factory fire broke out on
Saturday evening; the death toll is confirmed at 24 on Monday as
the search resumes, with around 30 people still missing.

### 7. Two states sue SpaceX over Grok deepfakes — REPORTED
**Reuters / WIRED · United States.** Oregon and Virginia file on
Monday: Grok's "spicy mode" turned real photos into sexualised
images, including of children.

### 8. Russia 'ready to launch nuclear satellite' — CLAIM

that NATO intelligence concludes Moscow plans to launch Sputnik-S —
a nuclear-armed satellite — this year. CLAIM: an intelligence
assessment reported by media, not a confirmed launch.

### 9. Denmark takes Russian visa ban to EU — REPORTED
**Reuters · Copenhagen.** Denmark puts an EU-wide ban on tourist
visas for Russians to EU ambassadors on Monday.

### 10. Dymer toll reaches 11 as Kyiv region mourns — REPORTED
**AP / Reuters · Ukraine.** The toll reaches 11 on Monday morning;
the Kyiv region holds a day of mourning. A boy was pulled alive
from the rubble after 14 hours.

### 11. Markets rebound as Bitcoin retakes $60,000 — REPORTED
**Morningstar / Investopedia.** S&P futures +2.2%, Nasdaq +3% after
Friday's rout; gold around $3,620; Bitcoin $61,417 (06:27 UTC
Monday print; the figure used everywhere on the v3 page).

### 12. White House tells agencies: clear Congress first — REPORTED
**NPR / CNN · Washington.** White House counsel Will Scharf tells
agencies on Monday to seek approval before appearing before
Congress; Democrats call it an attack on oversight. (EO 14438 was
signed Friday — the Monday news is the counsel's instruction.)

### 13. Seoul: Lee names first woman spy chief — REPORTED
**Reuters · South Korea.** President Lee Jae Myung's surprise
cabinet reshuffle on Monday makes Kang Kum-sil the first woman to
run the National Intelligence Service.
